Step 1 of 3

Create your personal Strava app

Strava caps a shared app at one connected athlete, so we sidestep that by each having our own — it's free, takes a minute, and only you ever see the credentials.

Tap the button below to open Strava's API settings page in a new tab. Log in with your usual Strava account, then fill in the form using the exact values listed (tap any value here to copy it). Keep that Strava tab open after you create the app — Step 2 reads your Client ID and Secret from it.

After you hit Create, Strava will ask for an app icon — upload anything (a wine bottle, your face, whatever). It's required but we never see it.

Step 2 of 3

Paste your two credentials

Switch back to the Strava API tab you opened in Step 1. Right at the top of that page, under "My API Application", you'll see your Client ID (a 6-digit number) and a hidden Client Secret. Click Show next to the secret to reveal the 40-character string, then paste both into the fields below.

Lost the Strava tab? Reopen Strava API settings ↗ — your app is still there, you don't need to recreate it.

The button sends you to Strava, where you click Authorize to grant read-only access to your runs. Strava then redirects you back to this page — Step 3 happens automatically.
